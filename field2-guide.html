Field 2 Scoreboard

Hope Hanks Park — Dripping Springs Youth Sports Association

🎉 Back in action!
As of May 2026, the Field 2 scoreboard is operational again after several years of inactivity!
Please feel free to run the scoreboard for your game. Please report any issues using the contact info below.
Scoreboards on all other fields are NOT currently operational. Please do not remove this controller.

NOTE: Balls and outs are not currently working correctly. The scoreboard can track runs and innings for now. We hope to have the full functionality restored in the future!

Before You Start

  1. The controller is stored inside the metal box on the home dugout side. Simply turn the latch and the front of the box will tilt down. The scoreboard should power on when the box is opened.
  2. Ensure the 5-pin DIN connector cable is plugged firmly into the port on the top of the controller.
  3. Flip the power switch on the top of the controller to ON. The scoreboard should display flashing 1s.
  4. Press BASEBALL to sync the controller with the scoreboard.
  5. Press and hold NEW GAME for 3 seconds to reset everything to zero.

Button Reference

HOME SCORE +/−Add/subtract home run
GUEST SCORE +/−Add/subtract guest run
INNINGAdvance inning number
NEW GAME (hold)Full reset, all zeros

Running a Half-Inning

  1. Run scored: press HOME SCORE + or GUEST SCORE +.
  2. If you accidentally add too many points, press the red -1 Home or -1 Guest buttons in the bottom left corner.
  3. At the end of the full inning (after the home team bats): press INNING to advance.
  4. There is no "-1 Inning" button. If you advance too early, press INNING multiple times to cycle up to 9 and back to the correct number.

After the Game

  1. Turn the controller power switch OFF.
  2. Leave the DIN connector plugged in to the controller.
  3. Close the box door and turn the latch to secure. The scoreboard will power off when the box is closed.
  4. Tell your friends the scoreboard is working, and offer to show someone else for the next game!

Troubleshooting

  • Flashing numbers (all "1"s) on startup: Normal. Press BASEBALL, then hold NEW GAME to initialize.
  • Scoreboard not responding: Check the power switch inside the control box — make sure it's in the ON position.
  • Controller not pairing: Power off the controller, wait 5 seconds, power back on. It should reconnect automatically.
  • Scoreboard is blank: The scoreboard may need its power cycled at the breaker. Please email us below.

Report an Issue

Equipment problems? Email pat@patcox.net with a description of the issue and the field number. This is an active restoration project!
