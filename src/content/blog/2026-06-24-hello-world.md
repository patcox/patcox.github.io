This site has been a placeholder for too long.

For years, `patcox.net` redirected people to my LinkedIn. That's fine, but LinkedIn doesn't really capture what I'm thinking about day to day — especially now, when AI is changing everything and I'm spending most of my time helping people figure out what it means for them.

So here we are.

## What you'll find here

I'll write about AI and automation — what's real, what's hype, and what's actually useful for people running businesses or trying to do their work better.

I'll probably also write about the other things I think about: rural life, community, technology as a human endeavor rather than a corporate product.

No editorial schedule. No algorithm to feed. Just writing when I have something worth saying.

## The scheduling page

One thing I built here that I'm genuinely excited about: [a page to schedule time with me](/connect). Meet up in person, or hop on a quick call. I spend a lot of time on Zoom but I'd rather be meeting people in person.

If you're in the Hill Country or just passing through Dripping Springs, let's get lunch.
